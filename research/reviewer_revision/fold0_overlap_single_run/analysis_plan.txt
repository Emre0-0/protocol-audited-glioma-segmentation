Development fold 0: controlled longitudinal overlap

Primary estimand: mean change in official six-region lesion-wise Dice
(exposed-training model minus archived clean model) on one fixed index scan
for each of the 40 deliberately exposed patients. Positive values favor
the exposed-training model. Use the final epoch (250) for both models;
do not choose a checkpoint or subgroup after inspecting the scores.

Control contrast: estimate the same change on one index scan per unexposed
longitudinal patient (n=39). Report the exposed-group change minus this
control-group change, including both component estimates. This helps
distinguish an exposure-specific pattern from a shift affecting both groups.
It is not a replication across independently trained model pairs.

Secondary summaries: six regional Dice changes, all 103 index scans, and
all 183 retained evaluation scans. The last summary weights scans equally
and resamples patients with their scans kept together. For each contrast,
use 10,000 patient bootstrap draws with the models paired within scan.
Resample the two disjoint groups independently for the contrast of changes.
Report percentile intervals; these describe patient sampling conditional
on the two fitted models, not variability across training seeds. Regional
and subgroup findings are descriptive, not separately confirmed effects.

Neither the 40 inserted sibling scans nor the retrospective holdout enters
any evaluation denominator. Both training arms have the same scan and
patient counts, but the 40 swaps change training composition and the old
initialization was not recovered. Do not equate this estimate with the
historical performance gap or claim that an interval spanning zero proves
there was no leakage effect.
